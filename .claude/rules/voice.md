# Voice

**Status: skeleton — fill this in yourself.**

The page that specifies this workspace names `voice.md` but does not define its contents,
and a voice rule written by an assistant would be a guess at how you write. A fabricated
voice is worse than no voice rule: it silently steers every draft toward a style you never
chose. So the sections below are prompts, not prescriptions. Replace each one.

The fastest way to fill this honestly: take two or three pieces you have written that you
were happy with, and describe what they do. Do not describe what good writing does in
general — describe what *yours* does.
