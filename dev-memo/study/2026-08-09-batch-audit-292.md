## Why the fresh-process variant was necessary, demonstrated not argued

MUTATION EVIDENCE: making the real capture vary per process (`width + getpid()`) failed 7 fresh-process
tests / 75 assertions **while the pre-existing in-process suite still passed 23/23 and the CLI still
printed `pass ok 2`**. The weaker test was blind to precisely the failure mode it was meant to detect.
Three further mutations (deleting the byte-exact raw check; dropping the child exit-status guard;
hardcoding the build path) each failed their guarding tests. All four reverted and sha256-verified
identical.

Writes nothing, verified under `sandbox-exec`: with file creation/unlink/truncate denied all modes still
pass; under a blunter deny-all-writes profile fresh-process FAILS CLOSED rather than silently passing.
