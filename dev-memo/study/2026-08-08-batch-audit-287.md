## Design decision recorded this window

Inline action order was a genuine fork: the approved spec said `[primary, cancel]` while macOS HIG puts
the default button rightmost, in a macOS-only app with a native-feel goal. Routed to Codex at the user's
direction (`review-plan-mskgim15-vhefre`) and then judged, not deferred to. Adopted: **INLINE surfaces
`[primary, cancel]`; a future MODAL/sheet follows HIG `[cancel, primary]`.** Mechanism: HIG's ordering
governs modal surfaces that stop the user to force a choice; these are inline task controls read
left-to-right. Destructive rows deliberately do NOT reverse — that would make placement a hidden safety
signal, when the real guard is the mandatory typed reason (hardened in `e9ff43c`).
